id: art-10
title: Division
---

*A disagreement that cannot be resolved within the Republic is resolved by
division. This Article states in advance how that is done, so that division is
an act of the Constitution and not a failure of it.*

## § 54  Right of Division

¹ Any twenty citizens, or one fifth of the citizens, whichever is fewer, MAY declare a division.

² A declaration states the ground of division and the name the departing Republic will take.

³ No organ of the Republic MAY prevent a division, delay it, or impose a condition upon it.

## § 55  Effect

¹ On division, the declaring citizens take a complete copy of the register as it stands at the moment of declaration.

² Both Republics succeed to that history. Neither is the continuation of the other, and neither MAY describe the other as illegitimate in its own Journal.

³ Records made after the declaration belong to the Republic that made them.

## § 56  Names and Symbols

¹ The departing Republic MUST take a distinct name and distinct symbols within thirty days.

² Until it does, it publishes under a provisional designation.

## § 57  Persons

¹ A citizen MAY hold citizenship of both Republics, or of either, and elects within thirty days.

² A citizen who does not elect is a citizen of the Republic in which they were admitted.

## § 58  Obligations

¹ Holdings of the unit and of instruments are mirrored in both Republics at the moment of division.

² An office held in the Republic is not held in the departing Republic, which fills its own offices afresh.

³ A judgment given before the division binds both Republics.

## § 59  Reunion

¹ Two Republics descended from a common division MAY reunite by a measure of the class of amendment carried in each.

² The terms of reunion, including the reconciliation of registers, are stated in the measure.
