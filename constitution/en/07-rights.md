*The provisions of this Article are not executable and MUST NOT be reduced to
a rule enforced by a tool. They bind the Republic and are enforced by the
Court.*

## § 33  Dignity and Equality

¹ Every citizen is equal in the Republic and enjoys its equal regard.

² No citizen MAY be discriminated against on any ground listed in Article 3 § 16 ⁵.

## § 34  Participation

¹ Every citizen has the right to speak in the Assembly, to propose, to vote, to stand for office, and to form an entity.

² No citizen MAY be excluded from the register, the Journal, or any public part of the Republic.

³ A citizen deprived of access is deprived of citizenship, and § 18 applies.

## § 35  Expression

¹ Every citizen MAY freely express and receive opinions and information in the Republic, being answerable for the abuse of that freedom.

² The Republic MUST NOT remove a published record on account of the opinion it contains.

³ This section does not protect harassment, threats, deliberate falsehood about a person, or content unlawful where the Republic's records are held.

## § 36  Due Process

¹ No citizen MAY be removed, sanctioned, or deprived of a permission except by a reasoned decision, after notice and an opportunity to answer.

² A citizen has the right to appear before the Court, to know the case, and to appeal.

## § 37  Privacy

¹ The Republic collects of a person only what is necessary, and only for a stated purpose.

² Personal data is held apart from the register, and the register refers to a person only by their pseudonymous identifier.

³ Every query against a person's data is itself recorded and is visible to that person.

## § 38  Erasure

¹ Every person MAY require the erasure of their personal data.

² Erasure removes the link between the person and their identifier. It does not remove any record, and Article 2 § 9 is not thereby breached.

³ After erasure the acts of that citizen remain in the register under their identifier alone.

## § 39  Exit

¹ Every citizen MAY depart, and MAY take with them a complete copy of the register.

² No provision of this Constitution, and no statute, MAY impede a departure or condition it on anything.

## § 40  Limitation

¹ A right in this Article MAY be limited only by organic statute, only so far as necessary in a self-governing association, and only to the extent proportionate to the purpose.

² The burden of justifying a limitation lies on the organ asserting it.

³ No limitation MAY negate the essential content of a right.

⁴ § 39 admits of no limitation.
