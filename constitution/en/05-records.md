id: art-05
title: Records and the Journal
---

## § 24  Record

¹ A record is an entry in the register attesting an act.

² Every record states its time, its author, its kind, the provision under which it is made, and its content.

³ The form of records is fixed by organic statute.

## § 25  The Journal

¹ The Republic publishes a Journal, in which every act of the Republic appears.

² Publication in the Journal is the promulgation of an act. An act not published has no effect.

³ Issues of the Journal are numbered consecutively and are never withdrawn.

## § 26  Verification

¹ The Republic SHALL publish the tools by which its register may be verified, and SHALL publish them under a licence permitting any use.

² Any person MAY operate a monitor, which verifies each published checkpoint against the last.

³ The Republic SHALL NOT require permission, registration, or identification to verify.

## § 27  Openness

¹ Every record, register, charter, judgment, and tally is public.

² A record MAY be withheld only where organic statute so provides, only to protect a person, and only for a stated period.

³ The fact of a withholding is itself published.
