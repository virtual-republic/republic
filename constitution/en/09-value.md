title: Value
---

## § 48  Unit

¹ The unit of account of the Republic is fixed by organic statute.

² The unit has no value outside the Republic. It MUST NOT be sold, purchased, redeemed, exchanged for anything of value, or represented as an investment.

³ No person MAY give anything of value to obtain the unit, and the Republic MUST NOT give anything of value in return for it.

## § 49  Issue

¹ The unit is issued only by the Treasurer, only under a resolution of the Assembly, and only in the amount that resolution states.

² Every issue is recorded and published.

³ Article 2 § 12 applies to every issue.

## § 50  Accounts

¹ Every citizen and every entity MAY hold one or more accounts.

² A transfer is a record and takes effect when recorded.

³ No account MAY be frozen except by order of the Court, for a stated period, on published reasons.

## § 51  Instruments

¹ An entity MAY issue instruments representing a share in it.

² An instrument is transferable between accounts and is recorded in the register.

³ § 48 ² applies to instruments as it applies to the unit.

## § 52  Exchange

¹ The Republic maintains an exchange for the transfer of instruments.

² The exchange clears by periodic auction at a uniform price, and MUST NOT give priority to the order of arrival within a period.

³ Every order, every clearing, and every price is published.

## § 53  Revenue and Treasury

¹ The Assembly MAY levy assessments on holdings and transfers, by organic statute, at rates the statute fixes.

² Assessments are computed by the published tool from the published rules, and the computation is published with the result.

³ The Treasury holds the Republic's account. Nothing leaves it except under an appropriation carried by the Assembly and citing this section.
