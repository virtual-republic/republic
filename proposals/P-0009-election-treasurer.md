## § 1  The office

¹ The office of Treasurer is filled under Article 6 § 29 ¹.

² The office is held for one year from the declaration of the result.

## § 2  Method

¹ The vote is by the single transferable vote in its instant-runoff form — Article 8 § 46 ¹.
