## Text

1. A Commune is a political body of citizens and a legal person.

2. The Charter of a Commune SHALL be a fork of the repository but MAY edit the details of the repository, except §§ 8-12 of the Constitution MUST apply to the Commune.

3. Communes MUST follow all law.

4. Communes MAY declare independence unilaterally from the Republic.
