## Text

1. The unit of account of the Republic is the **obol**, divided into one
   hundred **mina**.

2. The obol has no value outside the Republic. It shall not be sold,
   purchased, redeemed, or exchanged for anything of value, and shall not be
   represented as an investment.

3. Citizens receive obols for holding office, serving on the Court, convening
   an entity, and attending a session of the Assembly, at rates the Assembly
   fixes annually.

## Reasons

Article 9 § 48 ¹ leaves the unit to organic statute, and Article 1 § 4 ³
requires that rates and denominations sit in statute rather than in the
Constitution. This statute supplies both.
