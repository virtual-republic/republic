## § 1

¹ 1. The Department of Legal Affairs (DLA) is established and led by an elected Secretary.
2. DLA is responsible for:
    a. Writing and proposing bills;
    b. Ensuring the enforcement of laws;
    c. Ensuring the operation of the legal system.
