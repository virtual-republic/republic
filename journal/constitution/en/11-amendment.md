## § 60  Amendment

¹ This Constitution is amended by a measure of the class of amendment.

² A measure of amendment MUST set out the text to be inserted, altered, or removed, and MUST cite the provisions affected.

³ A measure of amendment MUST be carried twice, in two votes separated by not fewer than thirty days, with no alteration between them.

## § 61  Entrenched Provisions

¹ Articles 2, 7, and 11 are entrenched.

² An amendment to an entrenched Article is a measure of the class of entrenched provision.

³ Article 7 § 39 and Article 10 § 54 MUST NOT be amended so as to impede exit or division.

## § 62  Version

¹ Every amendment states the version it produces and the date from which that version has effect.

² Every version of this Constitution remains published.

³ An act is read against the version in force when it was made.

## § 63  Revision

¹ Not less than once in five years, the Assembly SHALL consider whether provisions of this Constitution have become statutory in character within the meaning of Article 1 § 4 ³.

² Provisions so found are removed by a measure of amendment and re-enacted as organic statute, without alteration of their effect.

## § 64  Commencement

¹ This Constitution takes effect on its publication in the first issue of the Journal.

² The founding citizens are those named in the first record of the register.

## § 65  Sufficiency

¹ No provision of this Constitution requires more than one citizen to be given effect.

² Where a provision speaks of citizens in the plural, it is satisfied by one.

³ A Republic of one citizen is a Republic.
