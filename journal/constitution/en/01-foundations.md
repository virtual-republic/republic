## § 1  The Republic

¹ The Republic is a voluntary civic association of its citizens.

² It is not a state, claims no sovereignty, and holds no territory.

³ Citizenship of the Republic confers no status under the law of any state and alters no obligation of any person.

⁴ The Republic belongs to no ancestry, faith, or tradition.

## § 2  Purpose

¹ The Republic exists so that its citizens may govern themselves by a text of their own making.

² It secures to every citizen a voice in its decisions, a share in its offices, and an account of its acts.

³ It commits itself to openness in all things, and to plainness in its records.

## § 3  Authority

¹ All authority in the Republic resides in its citizens.

² Authority is exercised only where this Constitution or an instrument made under it confers it.

³ No office, organ, or entity holds authority not conferred, and none MAY confer authority it does not hold.

## § 4  Supremacy and Form

¹ This Constitution is the whole of the Republic's authority.

² An act inconsistent with it is of no effect.

³ No provision of this Constitution MAY prescribe a rate, a sum, a schedule, the name of an office-holder, or the detail of a procedure. A provision of that character takes effect as an organic statute.

⁴ This Constitution SHALL be read purposively, and where a provision bears more than one reading, the reading that better secures the rights in Article 7 prevails.

## § 5  The Register of Authority

¹ The Republic maintains one repository in which its Constitution, its statutes, its registers, its journal, and its ledger are kept.

² The repository is authoritative. Any other rendering of the Republic, including any application or website, is a client and is not authoritative.

³ The repository SHALL be public, and every citizen MAY hold a complete copy.

⁴ The Republic SHALL maintain the repository on not fewer than two independent hosts.

## § 6  Language

¹ The language of the Republic is English.

² This Constitution, the statutes, the Journal, and the register are kept in English, and the English text is authentic.

³ A translation may be published for convenience. A translation is not authentic and confers no right.

## § 7  Interpretation

¹ The Preamble forms part of this Constitution. It confers no right and imposes no duty.

² The keywords MUST, MUST NOT, SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, and MAY are used in this Constitution as defined in RFC 2119.

³ Headings and identifiers form part of this Constitution and MAY be cited.
