title: Preamble
kind: preamble
---

We, who have chosen one another, establish this Republic.

We hold no territory and claim no sovereignty. We are bound by no soil, no
birth, and no border, but by a text we have written and consented to keep.

We build in the open. What is done here is recorded; what is recorded is
published; what is published may be verified by anyone, member or stranger.
We ask to be trusted no further than we can be checked.

We accept that any of us may leave, and that all of us together may divide.
We provide for that in advance, so that a disagreement among us is a fork and
not a ruin.

We establish this Constitution as the whole of our authority and the limit
of it.
