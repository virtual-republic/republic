## § 19  Right of Formation

¹ Every citizen MAY form an entity. No permission is required.

² Formation is effected by a record adding the entity to the register, stating its type, its charter, its organs, and its founding members.

## § 20  Types

¹ The types of entity are: the **association**, the **commune**, the **company**, the **foundation**, and the **organ of the Republic**.

² Organic statute provides the template for each type and MAY create further types.

³ An entity has the capacities of its type and no others.

## § 21  Charter and Organs

¹ Every entity has a charter, which is a published document, and organs named in it.

² An organ is held by one or more citizens and holds only the authority the charter confers.

³ A charter MUST NOT be inconsistent with this Constitution.

## § 22  Capacity

¹ An entity MAY hold an account, issue instruments under Article 9, employ organs, hold property of the Republic, and appear before the Court.

² An entity MUST NOT be a citizen, MUST NOT vote, and MUST NOT hold an office of the Republic.

## § 23  Dissolution

¹ An entity is dissolved by its charter's procedure, by resolution of its members, or by judgment of the Court.

² On dissolution its holdings pass as its charter provides, and failing that, to the Treasury.
