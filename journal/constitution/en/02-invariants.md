*The provisions of this Article hold at all times and are enforced by the
Republic's tools. An act that would violate one does not fail; it does not
occur.*

## § 8  Attribution

¹ Every record MUST identify exactly one author.

² The author of a record is a citizen, or an organ of an entity acting through a citizen.

³ A record whose author cannot be verified is not received.

## § 9  Immutability

¹ A record, once committed, MUST NOT be altered.

² A correction is a new record that refers to the record corrected.

³ The removal of personal data under Article 7 § 38 is not an alteration of a record, and MUST NOT remove the record.

## § 10  Continuity

¹ Every record MUST carry the hash of the record preceding it.

² The Republic SHALL publish, at intervals of not more than one week, a signed checkpoint attesting the state of the register.

³ A checkpoint MUST be verifiable by any person, without permission and without an account.

## § 11  Citation

¹ Every act of the Republic MUST cite the provision under which it is made.

² An act citing no provision, or a provision that does not resolve, is not received.

## § 12  Conservation

¹ Value in the Republic is created only by an act of the organ holding the issuing authority, under Article 9.

² Value is not created or destroyed by a transfer.

³ No account MAY be debited except by its holder, by an assessment made under statute, or by an order of the Court.

## § 13  Plurality

¹ A citizenship is a civic person, not a natural person.

² One natural person MAY hold one or more citizenships. Each is distinct, acts separately, and votes separately.

³ Each citizenship MUST have a key that no other citizenship holds.

⁴ Citizenship MUST NOT be transferred, sold, or lent.

⁵ The Republic makes no claim that its citizenships correspond one to one with natural persons, and no count of citizenships is a count of persons.
