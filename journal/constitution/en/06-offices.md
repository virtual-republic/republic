## § 28  Offices

¹ The offices of the Republic are the **Registrar**, the **Keeper of the Journal**, the **Treasurer**, the **Auditor**, and the **Judges** of the Court.

² Organic statute MAY create further offices.

³ Each office holds an enumerated set of permissions and no others. The set is published.

## § 29  Tenure

¹ An office is held for one year and is filled by election under Article 8.

² A citizen MAY hold more than one office.

³ An office-holder MAY be recalled by the procedure for their election.

⁴ On vacancy, the Assembly appoints until an election is held.

## § 30  The Assembly

¹ The Assembly is all citizens.

² The Assembly makes statutes, elects to office, approves the budget, and amends this Constitution under Article 11.

³ The Assembly acts by the procedure in Article 8 and by no other means.

## § 31  The Court

¹ The Court consists of one or more Judges, elected under Article 8. Where no Judge is elected, its functions are exercised by the Assembly.

² The Court decides disputes arising under this Constitution, reviews acts for consistency with it, and construes the authentic language versions under Article 1 § 6.

³ The Court MAY halt an act within its enactment window, and MAY declare an act of no effect.

⁴ The Court MUST NOT transfer value, and holds no permission over the Treasury.

⁵ Every judgment is published, cites the provisions construed, and states reasons.

## § 32  Accountability

¹ Every exercise of a permission is recorded, with the office under which it was exercised.

² The Auditor SHALL report to the Assembly at least twice a year, and the report is published.

³ The permissions attaching to each office SHALL be re-approved by the Assembly annually, and lapse if they are not.
